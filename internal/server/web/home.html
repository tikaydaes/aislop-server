A community list of AI slop on YouTube

This service backs the InvidioSlop browser extension. People report individual videos as AI-generated filler, say what kind it is, and others confirm or contest the report.

How a video gets listed

  • Each signed-in person gives one judgement per video: slop or not, which parts are AI (voice, visuals, script, music) and how much.
  • Judgements are weighted by reputation. Reputation is earned by agreeing with many different established people, and lost by being wrong.
  • A video is listed once the weighted lead is clear. A channel is listed once enough of its videos are, reported by enough different people.

Accounts

Anyone can download the list without an account. Reporting needs an account, which is a passkey and a nickname: no email address and no password. There is no account recovery, so add a second passkey if you can.

Privacy

  • The list is downloaded in full, so the service never learns which videos you watch.
  • The extension may ask which @handle belongs to a channel ID and contribute answers it finds. This can be switched off in the extension.
  • Network addresses are only kept as salted hashes of the address range, to limit abuse. The salt is replaced every 30 days.

Think a channel or video is listed wrongly? Contest it, no account needed.
